deep-one.glb
  Sculpt: Deep_One_ver1.stl, supplied by the GM (a tabletop miniature; base removed, scaled to 1.95 m,
  reduced to 60k triangles, painted with vertex colours in Blender).
  Animation: Mixamo "Mutant Walking" (Adobe Mixamo), retargeted to a fitted Mixamo skeleton and made
  walk-in-place, arms damped to suit the sculpt.
  Attack: Mixamo "Mutant Punch" (Adobe Mixamo), retargeted to the same skeleton; limbs follow the
  source by direction only, elbows and wrists capped, feet kept on the floor.
