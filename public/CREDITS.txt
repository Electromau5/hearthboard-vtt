  "Deep One" by coremort — https://sketchfab.com/coremort
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/deep-one-a71d657f33c04d4f8be403cf019c380e
  Licence: CC BY 4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

props/dagon-statue.glb — Dagon on his plinth in the shrine under the derelict house's sewer:
  "Dagon - Lovecraft lore" by CMBC — https://sketchfab.com/CMBC
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/dagon-lovecraft-lore-2d96cd1308de4e2e8f0b1413e26a5448
  Licence: CC BY 4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
  Changes: decimated from 1.1 M to 70 k triangles, re-centred on its base, texture
  converted to WebP, meshopt-compressed; tinted to stone in the walkthrough.
  The same statue comes alive in the Black Archive bunks' dream (godot/dream.pck):
  decimated to 24 k triangles, rigged to a Mixamo skeleton with a tail added, and
  animated with Mixamo's "Swimming" and "Treading Water" clips.
